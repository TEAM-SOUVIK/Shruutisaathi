# ShrutiSaathi v1.0 release notes

Initial v1.0 release candidate.

## Highlights

- Live caption capture with speaker-aware transcript organization.
- Predefined speaker labels with automatic next-speaker advancement.
- Experimental local speaker separation using offline voice embeddings.
- Conversation gist generation with outcome-first prompt rules.
- Reply drafting from user intent and recent conversation context.
- Transcript correction, save, share, and delete controls.
- Private AI Pack setup flow with one-time model download/import support.
- App-private transcript storage with backup exclusion rules.
- Light and dark themes with phone/tablet-friendly responsive UI.

## Known limitations

- Captions may be inaccurate in noisy environments or with unsupported accents.
- Automatic speaker separation is experimental and improves with longer, clearer turns.
- Generated gist and reply drafts must be reviewed by the user.
- The app is not a medical device, hearing aid, emergency system, or legal transcription product.
